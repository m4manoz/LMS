# Multi-Tenant Enterprise Design

Given your background in enterprise SaaS platforms, I would design this as a **multi-tenant system** where a single platform serves many organizations while keeping their data isolated.

```
Platform
│
├── Ministry of Education
│
├── University A
│   ├── Faculty of Engineering
│   ├── Faculty of Science
│   └── Faculty of Management
│
├── School Group B
│   ├── School 1
│   ├── School 2
│   └── School 3
│
├── Corporate Training Company
│
└── Government Training Institute
```

Each tenant can have:

* Custom branding (logo, colors, domain)
* Separate users and roles
* Independent academic structures
* Course catalogs
* Certificates
* Grading systems
* Reports
* AI policies
* Integrations

## Long-Term Vision

Rather than building a conventional LMS, aim to create a **learning operating system**—a platform where live teaching, self-paced learning, AI tutoring, virtual labs, assessments, analytics, collaboration, and lifelong learning all work together. Such an architecture can support K–12 schools, universities, government training centers, and corporate learning while remaining adaptable for the next decade.
